# AI Manifesto

This collection is curated by a person. AI does the typing, not the choosing.

## How an entry gets in

1. I(Fynks), find the project myself. I look through GitHub, GitLab, Telegram groups and channels, Reddit threads, LSPosed repositories through Vector, and XDA.
2. I check the link before saving it. I look at the source, the license, recent activity, and what the project actually does. If I cannot confirm those things, it does not go on my list.
3. I keep my own list of links that passed that check. Nothing is added straight from a search result, a feed, or an automated scrape.
4. I give that list to an AI agent. Sometimes it runs locally, sometimes through AI Arena. Its job is mechanical: place each entry on the right page, in the right category, in the right order, with the right badges.
5. I read the pull request it opens. I compare the diff with my list: same links, correct section, correct tags, no extra entries, no invented descriptions.
6. I merge it only after that review. If something is wrong, I send it back or fix it by hand.

## What AI does not decide

- Which projects belong in the repo
- Whether a source or license is acceptable
- Compatibility, safety, or maintenance status
- Claims about what an upstream project does
