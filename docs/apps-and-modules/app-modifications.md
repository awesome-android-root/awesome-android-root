# App Patchers & Mods for Root

This category covers app patchers (including YouTube), social and browser mods, media and camera tools, signature and verification changes, and LSPosed modules. Most entries require root or LSPosed.

> [!NOTE]
> Clicking an entry opens its **Source Code** page (GitHub/GitLab etc.) for `FOSS` apps, otherwise the
> **Google Play Store** listing.

## App Patchers

- **[⭐ Morphe](https://morphe.software/)** - Patches supported apps to add features and customizations. `FOSS`
- **[Lucky Patcher](https://www.luckypatchers.com/)** - App patcher and modifier (use with caution). `Proprietary`
- **[NexAlloy](https://github.com/NexAlloy/NexAlloy)** - Applies Morphe and ReVanced-based patches to supported apps, including YouTube. Requires root. `FOSS` `[LSP]`
- **[NexFusion](https://github.com/a092devs/NexFusion)** - Applies runtime patches to supported apps without modifying or re-signing APKs. `FOSS` `[LSP]`
- **[ReVanced](https://revanced.app/)** - Patches apps such as YouTube and Spotify to add features and customizations. `FOSS`
- **[ReVanced Extended](https://github.com/NoName-exe/revanced-extended)** - Builds patched YouTube and YouTube Music apps for root and non-root installations. `FOSS` `[M]` `[K]`
- **[ReVanced Magisk Module by j-hc](https://github.com/j-hc/revanced-magisk-module)** - Builds patched app modules and non-root APKs using ReVanced or compatible patches. `FOSS` `[M]` `[K]`
