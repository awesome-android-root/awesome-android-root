# Customization Mods for Root

**Customization** tools for rooted Android devices - themes and visual mods, launchers, status bar &
navigation tweaks, gestures, fonts & emojis, notifications, lockscreen/AOD and screen & display apps and
Magisk/KernelSU/LSPosed modules.


> [!TIP]
> Must check [ROM & OEM customization](system.md#system-ui-framework) section for more theming and customization options.

## Fonts & Emojis

- **[Apple Fonts & Emoji](https://github.com/kvmy666/iosEmojis-Font-module)** - Replaces the system Latin, Arabic and emoji fonts with SF Pro, SF Arabic, Geeza Pro or Cocon and Apple Color Emoji, each toggleable from a WebUI. `Proprietary` `[M]` `[K]`
- **[Emoji Replacer](https://play.google.com/work/apps/details?id=com.htetz.emojireplacer)** - Replaces system emojis with styles from iOS, Samsung, Google and other sources. `Proprietary`
- **[EvilFont](https://github.com/dedeadend/EvilFont)** - Replaces Arabic and Persian fonts with Teshrin and installs iOS emojis. `FOSS` `[M]` `[K]` `[A]`
- **[Font Manager](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.androidacy.fontmanager)** - Changes system fonts and emojis. `Proprietary`
- **[FontCraft Pro](https://github.com/RipperHybrid/FontCraft)** - Installs custom fonts and emoji packs systemlessly from a root-manager WebUI. `FOSS` `[M]` `[K]` `[A]`
- **[G-Font Installer](https://xdaforums.com/t/development-magisk-module-g-font-installer.4617743/)** - Installs Google fonts used by Google apps and Pixel devices. `Proprietary` `[M]`
- **[JetBrains Font](https://github.com/Mars-Wave/jetbrains-font-magisk-module)** - Installs the JetBrains Mono font systemlessly. `FOSS` `[M]`
- **[KernelSU-iOS-Emoji](https://github.com/n4bi10p/Ios-emoji)** - Systemlessly replaces emoji font with iOS Emoji. `FOSS` `[K]`
- **[Magisk-iOS-Emoji](https://github.com/Keinta15/Magisk-iOS-Emoji)** - Systemlessly replaces the emoji font with iOS Emoji. `FOSS` `[M]`
- **[MakeFontsGreatAgain](https://github.com/Numbersf/MakeFontsGreatAgain)** - Replaces system fonts with configurable Unicode ranges and Google Play Services masking. `FOSS` `[M]` `[K]`
- **[MFFMv14](https://github.com/mistu01/MFFMv14)** - Builds flashable font modules from user-supplied fonts, with automatic fixes for lockscreen clock colons, digit-width jitter and OEM quirks. Android 15+, built from a PC or Termux. `FOSS` `[M]` `[K]` `[A]`
- **[Nastaliq Urdu Font](https://xdaforums.com/t/module-font-nastaliq-urdu-font.4645787/)** - Adds a Nastaliq font for Urdu. `FOSS` `[M]`
- **[Noto Unicode Fallback](https://github.com/anemia004/noto-unicode-fallback-magisk)** - Adds Noto Sans Math and Symbols 2 as fallback fonts for missing Unicode symbols. `FOSS` `[M]`

- **[OneUI Emoji Pack](https://github.com/aloozchips/OneUIEmojiPack)** - Systemlessly replaces the AOSP emoji pack with the OneUI emoji pack. `FOSS` `[M]` `[K]` `[A]`
- **[Twemoji-Remastered](https://codeberg.org/Snowy/Twemoji-Remastered)** - Systemlessly replaces your phone emojis with Twemoji (Twitter Emoji). `FOSS` `[M]`
- **[Unicode Font Set](https://github.com/Losketch/UnicodeFontSet-magisk-module/blob/main/README.en.md)** - Installs Unicode fonts and their system configuration files. `FOSS` `[M]` `[A]`
- **[zFont 3](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.htetznaing.zfont2&hl=en)** - Changes fonts on supported Samsung, Xiaomi, OPPO and other Android devices. `Proprietary`

- **[Pixel Launcher Enhanced](https://github.com/Mahmud0808/PixelLauncherEnhanced)** - Customizes home-screen grids, dock, folders and other settings in Pixel Launcher and Launcher3. `FOSS` `[LSP]`

- **[Dressroom Advanced](https://github.com/BlueOutside/Dressroom-Advanced)** - Adds clock, widget and Now Bar controls to Samsung's Dressroom lock screen editor on One UI 9, with partial 8.5 support, plus variable font imports, live previews and separate cover and inner screen layouts. Android 16+. `Proprietary` `[LSP]`
- **[HyperGlow](https://github.com/amarinne/hyperglow)** - Animated lock screen and always-on display lyrics for HyperOS 3. `FOSS` `[LSP]`
- **[HyperLyric](https://github.com/limczhh/HyperLyric)** - Shows system lyrics in the status bar or HyperOS super island and restyles media cards. Android 13 to 16; lyrics need a separate source module. Upstream of HyperLyrics Enhanced. `FOSS` `[LSP]`
- **[HyperLyrics Enhanced](https://github.com/juren233/HyperLyrics-Enhanced)** - Shows HyperIsland and always-on display lyrics on HyperOS, with Apple Music enhancements and bundled Lyricon providers. `FOSS` `[LSP]`
- **[XMorph Clock](https://github.com/Mahmud0808/XMorphClock)** - Replaces the stock lock screen clock with animated morphing faces, weather, widgets and user-placed elements with separate layouts for the large clock, small clock and always-on display.
