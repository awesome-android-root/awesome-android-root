# Android General Guides & Tutorials

Task-focused how-tos for Android app management and system changes. Check each guide's prerequisites; some procedures require root, while others use ADB or Shizuku.

## Privacy & Ad Blocking

- **[Android Ad Blocking](./android-adblocking.md)** - Configure root-based hosts and DNS blockers with AdAway or Bindhosts. The guide covers setup, troubleshooting, and compatibility.

## App Management

- **[Safely Debloat Android Apps](./android-apps-debloating.md)** - Review and remove pre-installed apps with App Manager (root or ADB) or Canta (Shizuku); includes safety and restoration guidance.
- **[Stop Play Store Auto-Updates for Selected Apps](./stop-android-app-auto-updates-play-store.md)** - Use Zygisk Detach on a rooted device to keep selected apps from updating through Play Store. Detached apps will not receive Play Store security updates.
